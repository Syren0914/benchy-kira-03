## Publication preparation

The originals are in a public Drive folder, available to anyone with the link as a viewer. Public access was verified after the creator requested it. This package selects and links them; it does not contain exported photo copies. Export selected originals as sRGB JPEG or WebP for the public repository, preserve originals separately, and retain a filename-to-source mapping. Use roughly 1800–2400 px on the long edge for guide images, with sensible compression and readable labels. These are editorial targets, not platform requirements.

For IMG_1258, crop away the visible backdrop edges while preserving the full enclosure. A portrait or square crop works for the guide. For a landscape video thumbnail, place the product on one side and a short title on the other; avoid a crop that cuts off terminals. Inspect the original at full size for focus, dust, reflections, and clipping. Remove location metadata from publication copies. Do not retouch connectors, wiring, labels, or readings in a way that misrepresents the hardware.

The display is dark in the selected front preview. Use this photo as an appearance shot, not proof of operation. For the YouTube thumbnail, a newly photographed powered unit with a readable display and a visible test setup may communicate the project more effectively.

## Missing instructional photographs

1. All parts laid out with BOM item labels.
2. Supply and display-module manufacturer/model labels.
3. Empty enclosure and every mounting part.
4. Each mechanical assembly stage before it is concealed.
5. Internal wiring overview, safely disconnected, with wire IDs matching the schematic.
6. Connector mating-face views showing pin numbering and polarity.
7. Protective-earth, input protection, barriers, and cable-retention details appropriate to the actual design.
8. Finished internal assembly before closure.
9. Powered display beside an external meter and a known load, with test conditions recorded.
10. Thermal and combined-load test setup.

Exterior photographs cannot replace a schematic or connector schedule. Photograph electrical assembly only under a safe, de-energized service procedure.
